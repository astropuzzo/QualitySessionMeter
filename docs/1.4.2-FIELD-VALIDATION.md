# QSM 1.4.2.0 field validation

Validation date: 2026-09-21. Release validation for 1.4.2.0. Original science files were not renamed or changed. All 52 LIGHT sizes and modification timestamps match the pre-analysis inventory; FLATs were excluded.

## Revised decision model

A normal gradual signal change must not be rejected just because it differs from the beginning of the night. Timestamped clean references follow a coherent local trend; the initial signal is used only for a separately configurable minimum (40% by default). Unexpected signal loss plus fewer detected stars can reject whether the sky brightens or darkens. Warnings during a suspicious change do not train the reference. Forecasts through gaps are bounded, and rejected frames never become new references.

Matched photometry remains available when shape classification fails. Unresolved single-pixel detections no longer count as failed shape measurements. Guide recovery still requires sufficient shape evidence, including external regions when needed. Borderline or incomplete evidence is not described as confirmed damage.

## Validation and limits

- 142 assessment/image/reference/storage checks, including sustained gradual decline/increase, irregular cadence, sudden dark-sky loss, rapid multi-frame loss, cloud plateau, recovery, configurable floor and missing evidence.
- 13 previous-night shape assertions; 33 September 20 checks; retained September 13 image/signal assertions and revised policy checks.
- Public and OpenAstro inspector JavaScript checks, plus desktop/mobile renders at 1440 and 430 pixels. Original/new selectors and proof images verified without script errors or horizontal overflow.
- Legacy synthetic regression: six profiles, 108 frames, zero failures. WPF cross-dispatcher checks passed.
- Native Options rendered with report destination, actual path/status and minimum session signal.
- Session destination checks cover default/custom/beside-LIGHT, persistence, fixed active destination, next-session changes and fallback on unavailable paths.

This is a chronological replay of two nights from one optical setup, not a live acquisition validation or a general accuracy estimate. Thresholds were not tuned to a target acceptance percentage. Default minimum signal is relative to initial references, not absolute SNR; an initially poor session can still require human review. Catalog thresholds and scatter bounds are engineering criteria, not calibrated weather probabilities.
