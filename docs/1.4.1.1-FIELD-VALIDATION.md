A live acquisition run of this version is not part of the recorded validation. Supersedes the 1.4.1.0 candidate, whose apparent recovery results missed accepted signal-loss cases.

Thus frame 1010 retained a signal loss of about 36% relative to its already-degraded rolling reference. Several late frames lost much more against a preceding clear exposure.

## Changes

Direct matched-signal rejection and a corroborated sky/count/signal rule are independently switchable from count-flag verification. Defaults: direct signal loss above 35%; combined loss at least 20%, background rise at least 3%, and count loss at least half the configured count limit (minimum 10%). Early review thresholds protect references before hard rejection. Past clean references are retained for up to 120 minutes, still bounded by the selected window and isolated by imaging context and pier side. A cleared guide flag cannot clear signal degradation. These are configurable relative-quality criteria, not a calibrated weather classifier.
